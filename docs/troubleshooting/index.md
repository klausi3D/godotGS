# Troubleshooting

**For:** anyone whose godotGS editor, scene or exported game does not show splats the way it should.

**At the end:** you have matched your symptom to a fix on [Recurring Issues](recurring-issues.md), or you know what to put in a bug report.

Find your symptom:

- [The stock Godot editor opened instead of godotGS](recurring-issues.md#the-stock-godot-editor-opened-instead-of-godotgs)
- [Nothing renders](recurring-issues.md#nothing-renders)
- [Black or dark splats](recurring-issues.md#black-or-dark-splats)
- [Exported game renders no splats](recurring-issues.md#exported-game-renders-no-splats)
- [Slow on the Linux nightly](recurring-issues.md#slow-on-the-linux-nightly)
- [Crash or error when the game exits](recurring-issues.md#crash-or-error-when-the-game-exits)
- [Windows warns about the download](recurring-issues.md#windows-warns-about-the-download)
- [No nightly works on your machine](recurring-issues.md#no-nightly-works-on-your-machine)

Not listed? Check [Known Public Alpha Limitations](../development/known-public-alpha-limitations.md) and the [FAQ](../user/manual/faq.md), then [ask for help](recurring-issues.md#getting-help).
