3// GSQ page builder: the writer's codec stage (BV-C3; docs/architecture/adr-beyond-vram-worlds.md
4// D-12, §4.4 W12/W15, §6.2 "Per-chunk order").
5//
6// It turns one chunk, whose splats arrive in importance order (§6.2: a prefix of the chunk is a
7// valid sample), into validated GSQ v1 pages:
8//
9// 1. Page constants. The codec stores area, DC alpha, stroke age, brush axes, painterly_meta
10// and render_meta once per page and refuses a page whose splats differ in them
11// (gsq_heterogeneous_field). The builder therefore partitions the chunk into constant
12// groups, splats whose page constants are bit-identical, keeping importance order inside
13// each group. A chunk that needs more than one group is counted (heterogeneous_chunks,
14// constant_groups), never silently refused or rewritten.
15// 2. Page order follows importance (user decision 2026-11-08). Each group is cut into pages of
16// at most kWriterPageSplats consecutive splats of its importance order, and the pages of
17// the chunk are ordered by their first (most important) splat. A prefix of pages is
18// therefore a prefix of every group: prefix LOD stays valid, at whole-page granularity.
19// 3. Morton order only inside a page (decision 2026-11-07, #1347), where the codec delta-codes
20// positions. Keys are 3 x 21 bits of the order-preserving integer image of the position
21// floats relative to the page minimum, ties broken by importance rank. Integer only, so the
22// order cannot depend on -ffast-math or contraction (risk K10).
23// 4. W12: every page is encoded, its stored digest verified, decoded in full and semantically
24// validated against its source splats (finite fields, every attribute within its D-12
25// quantization step, page constants bit-identical) before it is handed out.
26// 5. W15: each page carries its stored digest and its decoded canonical digest.
27//
28// A page that still cannot be built (a field the codec refuses: non-finite, out of range, SH
29// degree 4) fails the whole chunk with the gsq_* reason and the chunk and page it hit; no page
30// of that chunk is returned. Nothing is dropped.
31//
32// Container placement (plain v3 bit 7) is BV-C5a; this stage produces the pages it will write.
33// Not thread-safe: one builder per thread (it owns a DecodeContext).
