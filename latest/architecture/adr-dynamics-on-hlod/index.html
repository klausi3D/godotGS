    ADR: Dynamics on HLOD streaming (static world tree, per-splat semantic word, LOD-stable wind, region overrides)¶

    • Status: Accepted (owner decisions, 2026-11-20). The owner approved the direction on 2026-11-19 ("without it the large-world architecture makes no sense for games"). On 2026-11-20 the owner accepted the recommended option of every open decision (O1–O5, O7–O10); O6 stays open with an interim rule. The decisions are recorded in §11, and the program decision with its kill criteria in §0. D1–D8 (§5) are accepted. The design detail marked [P] is what the work packages implement; each package carries its own evidence. Acceptance of this record does not establish implementation or runtime acceptance.
    • Date: 2026-11-20 (drafted 2026-11-19).
    • Risk class: the file is R0 (docs/**). It is the R3 design record for the work it describes. That work changes the persisted Gaussian layout (GAUSSIAN_STRUCT_LAYOUT_VERSION), the GSQ page codec, the HLOD merge key, the renderer and its shaders, and the public node API.
    • Builds on: adr-hlod-streaming.md (§6.5, §6.9, §14.1, §14.4.1, §14.5), adr-one-route-many-producers.md (§4.5, §4.6, §8 GR-02/03/07/09/15, §9 GS-COMBINED-1) and adr-v1-animation-collision-scope.md (D-6a: animation is kept and wired into rendering, through CLN-SH06; D-6b: collision proxies are baked, through CLN-SH04).
    • Tracking: Refs #1499 (shader identity keyed by atlas address), #1245 (SH cache shared across instances), #1424 (omni and spot lights cast no splat shadows on HLOD), #1473 (asset-set change resets world residency), #1500 (pre-M3 batch), #1312 (picking stub), #1502 (SH colour cache serves the previous tenant's colour after atlas page reuse; WP1), #487.
    • Inputs: an internal design check and deviation report (2026-11-19). They are not repository documents. Figures taken from them are marked as such.
    • Source baseline: origin/master = 265951b5ca2. Every file:line below was read at that commit. Paths are relative to modules/gaussian_splatting/ unless they start with docs/, tests/ or .github/.
    • Line drift at landing: this record lands on 9faecfb155a (#1500). Between the two commits, shaders/, compute/, lod/, nodes/, io/gsq/, core/gaussian_splat_hlod_merge.cpp and core/quality_tier_config.cpp are unchanged, so those citations hold. #1500 changed renderer/resident_instance_contract_publisher.cpp (publisher.cpp), renderer/tile_renderer.cpp and core/gaussian_streaming.h.     0. Program decision (owner, 2026-11-20)¶

    • HLOD is the route. Dynamics are built on the HLOD route described here. There is no return to the chunk-streaming route; route retirement stays as decided in one-route §12 (D-2).
    • Kill criteria. The architecture is re-opened if any of these is met:
    • Tier A does not settle on HLOD.
    • Frame p95 on the 3080 is above 25 ms, and profiling cannot explain it.
    • GS-GAME-1 (§7) cannot be reached with this architecture.
    • A kill criterion is met only by a measured result with its evidence. A run that did not happen, or that is INVALID, meets none of them and passes none of them.

    1. Problem¶

    The HLOD route is now the one route for streamed worlds (one-route §12). It draws the world and the node instances in one draw list and one sort [ADR], and it never draws an unsafe hole [ADR]. But for a game it is static.

    1. Wind or one effector un-streams the whole world.
    2. A V4 paged world is materialised into memory and handed to the resident route when project wind is on or the project has a sphere effector: nodes/gaussian_splat_world_3d.cpp:722-723 and :728-729 set hlod_paged_deformation_or_instance_domain, and :737 calls materialize_paged_resident [V].
    3. The same project-global test refuses the HLOD route in _hlod_auto_route_refusal() (nodes/gaussian_splat_world_3d.cpp:646, :657) and in GaussianSplatRenderer::hlod_streaming_unsupported_reason() (renderer/resident_instance_contract_publisher.cpp:1867-1869) [V].
    4. One waving bush is therefore enough to load a 55 GiB world into memory. For Tier A this means wind is impossible [I].
    5. Wind is not a game wind model.
    6. Stiffness is derived from opacity: "Reuse opacity as a rough 'stiffness' signal until a dedicated attribute exists" (shaders/includes/gs_deformation.glsl:238-240) [V].
    7. The phase gets a random offset of 0 to 2π per splat (gs_deformation.glsl:242-245) [V].
    8. The seed of that offset is the atlas address: atlas_index ^ instance_id * 0x9e3779b9 (compute/depth_compute.glsl:201) and gaussian_idx ^ ... (shaders/tile_binning.glsl:992), where gaussian_idx is the atlas index (tile_binning.glsl:896) [V].
    9. The splat shadow caster applies no deformation: shaders/gs_shadow_caster.glsl has no match for wind|deformation|effector [V].
    10. Nothing inflates a bound for the displacement: a search for a wind margin or inflation in the module finds nothing [V, search not exhaustive].
    11. Splats carry no semantics.
    12. The merge key is the cell coordinate plus the DC-encoding bit, and nothing else (core/gaussian_splat_hlod_merge.cpp:238-239) [V].
    13. Every other field of a merged splat is copied from the heaviest member: r_out = p_rep.gaussians[best] (merge.cpp:467) [V].
    14. The leaf's source index exists only during the bake (core/gaussian_splat_hlod_bake.cpp:564, :606) and is dropped (core/gaussian_splat_world.cpp:1051) [V].
    15. There is therefore no runtime way to say "this house", "this tree" or "this bush waves".
    16. No runtime recolour survives LOD and streaming.
    17. The highlight is one global uniform per renderer (shaders/includes/tile_raster_common.glsl:561-563) [V].
    18. The per-instance row has opacity, wind and effector fields but no colour (shaders/includes/gs_instance_layout.glsl:29-38) [V].
    19. Picking is a stub that returns no hit (interfaces/interactive_state_manager.cpp:169-177) [V].
    20. F1 edits replace at most 64 leaves (lod/hlod_edit.h:48), and the ancestors of an edited leaf go stale (lod/hlod_edit.h:7-12) [V]. A recolour through F1 therefore forces full detail and does not scale over distance [I].
    21. Shader state keyed by atlas address breaks under streaming.
    22. The wind seed, the sort tie-break (compute/depth_compute.glsl:271), the subpixel history (shaders/tile_binning.glsl:1182-1190) and the SH colour cache (shaders/tile_binning.glsl:1445) all index by atlas address [V].
    23. Atlas pages are reused: lod/hlod_node_residency.cpp:110 and :232 release slots, and :694 and :874 allocate them to the next node through the best-fit allocator (core/streaming_atlas.cpp:66-88) [V].
    24. The SH cache case is a live defect on master under SH amortization (§6.5). It is not only a future relocation hazard as #1499 states.

    This ADR decides how dynamics live on top of the HLOD design without changing the cut, the residency or the V4 page structure.

    2. Decision drivers¶

    1. Game first (owner, 2026-11-04): streamed world, static, dynamic and interactive objects, and lighting in one frame. No feature may silently make the world exclusive or resident.
    2. Every dynamic effect works at every LOD and survives eviction, readmission and compaction. An effect that depends on which atlas page a splat sits on is a defect.
    3. Re-bake at most once. A Tier A bake is 24,290 nodes and 9.87 GB (run 43288178399, from the deviation report). Changes to the codec or the bake sources invalidate the world cache (tests/runtime/flythrough/world_cache.py:84-103 lists io/gsq/gsq_codec.* among the generator sources; :105-120 lists the bake and auto-tree sources as tree sources) [V]. Every format-relevant change therefore goes into one format step.
    4. Fail closed, never drop silently. GSQ v1 already refuses heterogeneous page constants (io/gsq/gsq_codec.cpp:436-437) [V]. A new per-splat field must be carried or refused, never zeroed.
    5. Evidence integrity. Every acceptance row needs a control that fails on the old behaviour in the same job (docs/governance/evidence-integrity.md) [ADR].

    4. Options¶

    Option Summary Verdict
    A. Static tree plus a semantic word plus instances The world tree is static. Each splat carries a 32-bit semantic word (region ID, wind weight). Wind and effectors are functions of rest position and the word. Recolour goes through a region-indexed override table. Moving or deforming content is extracted into instances at bake time. Chosen [P]
    B. Stable per-splat identity in the shader (NodeKey, index) and per-splat override buffers Gives every splat an identity, plus side buffers keyed by it. Rejected: merged splats have no identity, so the effects would not hold across LOD. It also needs dense identity-keyed side buffers for billions of splats. Kept only for the sort tie-break (§6.5).
    C. Dynamic content in the tree, with F1/F2 rebake on change Moving or recoloured content stays in the world tree and is rebaked when it changes. Rejected: every change forces full detail and an upload, scales with the edit and not with the screen, and F2 does not exist [V lod/hlod_edit.h:11-12].
    D. Keep wind on the resident route only Wind stays where it works today. Rejected: it contradicts driver 1. A Tier A world cannot be resident [I].

    Practice elsewhere [Ext]: H3DGS and Octree-GS hierarchies are static. SC-GS drives motion as a field over a few control nodes, evaluated at the splat position. Nanite allows world-position offset (WPO) only with a declared Max WPO Displacement for cluster bounds, and Fortnite turns WPO off with distance. SpeedTree bakes wind weights at every LOD level. Unreal's World Partition HLOD covers static actors only. Every one of them separates bundled static content from movable content, and makes world dynamics a function of position plus a baked attribute with a declared bound. Option A does the same.

    5. Decision overview (accepted 2026-11-20)¶

    The owner accepted D1–D8 with the decisions in §11.

    # Decision Section
    D1 Authoring rule: the baked world tree is static. Anything that moves, deforms or is changed interactively per object is an instance. Dynamic islands are extracted at bake time. §6.1
    D2 Semantic word: 32 bits per splat (region ID, wind weight, reserved bits, a valid bit), persisted through layout version 2 and GSQ v2, uploaded into the existing GPU padding. The merge key never mixes regions. Wind weight is aggregated like colour. §6.2
    D3 Wind model: phase from rest position only (no identity jitter), baked wind weight instead of opacity, bound inflation from a declared max displacement, the caster deforms with the same time sample, a distance fade, and per-row opt-in instead of the project-global refusal. Delivered as an S–M step without a format bump first, then the format step. §6.3
    D4 Region overrides: a GPU table indexed by region ID (tint, emissive, opacity multiplier, flags), applied at shading time, in the depth pass for hide, and in the caster. A public API on world and instance nodes. §6.4
    D5 Atlas-keyed state: content-following caches (SH colour, subpixel history) are invalidated whenever an atlas run is (re)assigned. Identity values (wind phase, tie-break) no longer use the atlas address. This splits #1499 item 1. §6.5
    D6 Rigid objects: the shadow uses the current frame's rows (fixes the one-frame lag). A4/A5 are triaged before tolerances are set. Object LOD and proxies are out of scope for the first gate. §6.6
    D7 Skinning and deformation are out of scope, and go through the instance route in the CLN-SH06 animation render ADR. §6.7
    D8 GS-GAME-1 is the vertical-slice gate for "game alpha". §7

    6. Design¶

    6.1 Authoring rule: world versus instance (D1)¶

    Rule [P]: The baked world tree is static. Anything that moves, deforms or is interactively changed per object is an instance.

    Content Where it lives Why
    Terrain, buildings, rocks, the static parts of vegetation world tree static geometry; cut and streaming apply
    Vegetation that sways in place (grass, leaves, branches) world tree, with a baked wind weight the displacement is a bounded function of rest position and the word (§6.3); no identity needed
    Regions recoloured, highlighted, hidden or made emissive as a whole (a house turning red, a lit window, a quest marker) world tree, with a region ID the override table (§6.4) works at every LOD
    Local destruction of up to 64 leaves within leaf bounds world tree plus an F1 edit lod/hlod_edit.h:14-19 [V]
    Doors, vehicles, props, pickups, anything with a transform that changes instance (GaussianSplatNode3D) rigid transform per row; fully resident in the instance tail (publisher.cpp:1403-1418) [V]
    Falling trees, destructible houses, characters instance, plus a later deformation path §6.7
    Large continuous changes (a flood, seasonal repaint) out of scope would need a rebake (F2) or content swaps

    Bake-time extraction of dynamic islands [P]:

    1. Authoring marks islands with volumes (GaussianSplatDynamicIslandVolume3D, a box or convex hull plus an optional asset name) or with a per-splat source property (island_id in PLY).
    2. bake_hlod() and the auto-tree (D2-3 driver) remove the island splats before the leaf partition. Each island is written as its own asset (plain v3, or V4 when large) with an islands[] manifest in the world metadata: name, asset path, rest transform, bounds.
    3. A helper places one GaussianSplatNode3D per island at its rest transform. The game then moves, hides or swaps it like any other instance.
    4. Hole policy: a scan has no data behind an extracted object. The bake reports the area uncovered by the extraction, and authoring decides whether to fill it, which is a content-pipeline task and not engine work [I].
    5. Islands count against the instance tail and the shared ledger (one-route §4.5) [ADR]. Many islands need instance LOD and proxies (§6.6.3).

    6.2 Per-splat semantic word (D2)¶

    6.2.1 Layout [P]¶

    One uint32 per splat, the same bits everywhere it is stored:

    Bits Field Meaning
    0–15 region_id 0 = default region of the asset. 1–65,535 = authored regions. The scope is per asset, so world and object assets each have their own space.
    16–23 wind_weight unorm8: 0 = rigid, 255 = full sway.
    24–27 wind_class Reserved for a flutter or frequency band (trunk, branch, leaf). Written as 0 now; readers ignore it.
    28–30 reserved Must be 0. Readers refuse non-zero (a future meaning must not be misread).
    31 semantic_valid 1 = baked semantic data. 0 = legacy content: region 0, and wind falls back to the row weight (§6.3).

    Why 16 region bits: an override table with 65,536 entries × 32 B = 2 MiB is affordable resident [I]. 20 bits would cost 32 MiB per world. Decided: 16 bits (O3).

    Where it is stored [P]:

    Store Location Change
    Authoring Gaussian _padding @116 becomes uint32_t semantic GAUSSIAN_STRUCT_LAYOUT_VERSION 1 → 2, pin assert updated (core/gaussian_data.h:216, :244). Layout-1 files keep loading, and their word is forced to 0, not read: before #687 the padding was not reliably zero (comment at gaussian_data.h:150-158) [V].
    GSQ GSQ v2 (codec id 2): adds 4 byte planes of semantic per sub-block. The planes are spatially coherent, so zstd should compress them to near nothing [I, to be measured]. v1 stays readable, with the word = 0.
    V4 unchanged container; the payload record already names the struct layout [ADR §14.4.1] The node record gets max_wind_weight (u8) in a reserved word [ADR: "reserved words must be zero"; the exact word is not verified in code here].
    GPU raw PackedGaussian._pad_rotation_align pack_gaussian() writes src.semantic instead of 0u (gaussian_gpu_layout.cpp:236). The upload checksum already hashes the word (comment :232-235) [V].
    GPU quantized PackedGaussianQuantized._tail_padding[0] Same bits.

    6.2.2 Merge rules [P]¶

    1. Never merge across region IDs.
    2. The 64-bit sort key is full: three 21-bit cell coordinates plus bit 63 for the DC encoding (merge.cpp:20, :239) [V], so the region ID cannot be packed into it.
    3. Each sort entry instead gets a second field (region_id), compared after the cell key. For single-region content the order, and therefore the merge output, stays bit-identical to today except for the word itself [I; the test must show it].
    4. The region ID is inherited exactly. Cells are region-pure, so r_out = gaussians[best] (merge.cpp:467) already copies the correct ID [V for the copy; I for purity under rule 1].
    5. The wind weight is a weighted mean. The merge does not copy it from the winner. It uses the moment weights the merge already uses for colour, w = opacity × s_max × s_mid (merge.cpp:368) [V], and the result is rounded to the nearest unorm8.
    6. semantic_valid is the AND of the members. wind_class comes from the member with the largest summed weight.
    7. The node gets max_wind_weight = max over its payload (not over its subtree). It sets the node's displacement bound (§6.3.3).
    8. Interior nodes' compression cost:
    9. Region boundaries merge less, so interior nodes grow. The cost is not measured.
    10. Estimated single-digit percent for hundreds of regions [I, design check §1.2].
    11. It must be measured on T0 with the existing §5.2 setup before the Tier A rebake (acceptance row F3).

    6.2.3 Persistence and the timing constraint [P]¶

    • One format step (WP3, §8) carries all of: layout v2, GSQ v2, the merge key, the wind-weight aggregation, max_wind_weight, bake input for regions, wind weights and islands, and the auto-tree changes. These are exactly the sources the world cache keys on (tests/runtime/flythrough/world_cache.py:88-120) [V]. Landing them one by one would invalidate the Tier A cache several times.
    • Interaction with the auto-tree: tree_sha covers the codec block and the source fingerprint [ADR, core/gaussian_splat_hlod_auto_tree.h:40-48, V for the comment]. GSQ v2 changes the codec id, so every cached tree becomes stale by key. This is intended, and it must fail closed rather than serve a v1 tree [I].
    • Timing:
    • M3 (the Tier A fly-through) measures the cut, which the word does not change in kind.
    • Decided (O1): run M3 on the current format now; freeze format-relevant changes after M3; land WP3 as the single format step; then make the production Tier A bake once, on layout v2 and GSQ v2.
    • The rejected alternative, landing WP3 before M3, would have delayed M3 by an M-size R3 package.

    6.3 Wind model for games (D3)¶

    6.3.1 Displacement [P]¶

    d(x_rest, t) = dir × A_row × W(splat) × F(x_rest, t) × fade(distance, level)
F(x, t)      = sin(k·x.xz + ω·t + φ_noise(x)) (+ optional gust term)
    • Phase from rest position only.
    • φ_noise is low-frequency world-space noise (a hash of a coarse lattice cell, smoothly interpolated), never a per-splat hash.
    • The identity jitter (gs_deformation.glsl:242-243) is removed, and so are the seed parameters in depth_compute.glsl:201 and tile_binning.glsl:992 [V for current code].
    • LOD stability [I]:
    • A merged splat sits at the weighted mean of its members (merge.cpp:468) [V].
    • For a field whose wavelength is much longer than the node extent, its displacement equals the weighted mean of its children's to first order.
    • For coarse nodes this no longer holds, so fade drives the weight to 0 above a level or distance (Fortnite's WPO distance cutoff [Ext]). Coarse nodes with weight 0 are allowed.
    • W(splat):
    • With semantic_valid, it is the baked wind_weight.
    • Without it (legacy), a world row does not sway under active project wind (O4). Wind is opt-in per world: a legacy world that opts in, and every instance row, uses the row weight times today's opacity heuristic (gs_deformation.glsl:239-240). This keeps the resident route visually close for old content that asks for wind [P].
    • Effectors: the same rule applies. Their per-effector seed (stable_seed + i * 0x9e3779b9, gs_deformation.glsl:148) [V] is replaced by a position hash.
    • Identity flutter: an optional small leaf-level amplitude keyed by (node id, index in node). Decided: none for now (O5); revisit after GS-GAME-1.

    6.3.2 Removing the project-global refusal [P]¶

    • Wind and sphere effectors are removed from:
    • the paged fallback list (nodes/gaussian_splat_world_3d.cpp:722-723, :728-729);
    • _hlod_auto_route_refusal() (:646, :657);
    • hlod_streaming_unsupported_reason() (publisher.cpp:1867-1869) [V for the current sites].
    • The decision becomes per row:
    • The world row and every instance row carry their own wind_mode. The existing field is InstanceDataGPU.params.w (gs_instance_layout.glsl:33) [V].
    • The world row defaults to inherit only when the world has valid semantic data, otherwise off; a world opts in explicitly (O4, decided).
    • Effectors carry a row mask; the existing effect_params.z is the "scene effector mask bits" (gs_instance_layout.glsl:37-38) [V].
    • One-route §4.6 ("each domain is lifted in its own PR (F5)") [ADR]: this is that PR for wind and effectors. Painterly and the shadow instance filter stay refused.

    6.3.3 Bound inflation (Max displacement) [P]¶

    • D_max = strength × max(row intensity) × (1 + effector max push) is a frame-global bound, because |d| ≤ strength × intensity × resistance with resistance ≤ 1 (gs_deformation.glsl:240-246) [V].
    • Without the format step: every node of a wind-enabled row gets D_max added to its AABB for frustum culling and for the cut's screen-size and record estimates.
    • With the format step: the inflation is D_max × max_wind_weight(node) / 255.
    • When D_max changes, it changes the cut input. This is acceptable, because the cut already reacts to the camera every frame [I].
    • Cost [I]: slightly more overlap records and conservative culling. It must be measured in GS-GAME-1 (row P1).

    6.3.4 Shadow-caster consistency [P]¶

    • The caster (shaders/gs_shadow_caster.glsl, which today has no deformation [V]) includes gs_deformation.glsl and is given the same per-frame wind time (compute_wind_time_seconds, gs_project_settings.h:283-285) [V].
    • Its draw list may be the previous main view's (§6.6.1). Positions are still deformed with the current frame's time, so colour and shadow sway in phase.
    • The caster honours hide (§6.4).

    6.4 Runtime recolour, tint, emissive and hide (D4)¶

    Table [P]: - An SSBO RegionOverrideGPU[] of 32 B entries: tint.rgb, tint_strength, emissive.rgb, opacity_mul, flags (HIDE, NO_WIND, HIGHLIGHT, NO_SHADOW) and 4 bytes of padding. - Each asset gets a segment. The instance row's reserved lod.y (gs_instance_layout.glsl:35) [V] holds the segment base, with 0xFFFFFFFF for none. The splat's entry is table[base + min(region_id, segment_len - 1)]; entry 0 of a segment is the "whole asset" entry. - Uploads go as dirty ranges from the CPU and take effect in the same frame they are set, before the main view is recorded.

    Application points [P]:

    Effect Where Why there
    HIDE, opacity_mul == 0 compute/depth_compute.glsl, before the sort-key write (:268-273) [V for the location] Hidden splats cost no sort records (acceptance row R3).

    6.5.2 SH colour cache: stale colour after page reuse, verified mechanism [V], visibility [I]¶

    1. On the HLOD route gaussian_idx is the atlas record index (tile_binning.glsl:896) [V].
    2. The cached value is used whenever use_cache && !sh_force_update holds, the phase does not match, and the cached word is non-zero (tile_binning.glsl:1441-1449). There is no tag or key [V].
    3. sh_force_update (params.sh_config[3], renderer/tile_render_stages.cpp:331) is driven by sh_cache_needs_full_update [V]. That flag is set only by:

    5. a mode or divisor change (:607-608, :4165);
    6. an SH band change, which also clears the buffer (renderer/tile_render_stages.cpp:316-319). No residency event sets it. clear_color_cache has exactly one caller, the band change (tile_render_stages.cpp:318) [V].
    7. HLOD residency returns evicted runs to the allocator (lod/hlod_node_residency.cpp:110, :232) and hands them to the next admitted node (:694, :874). The allocator is best fit over freed runs (core/streaming_atlas.cpp:66-88) [V].
    8. The HLOD route does use the cache:
    9. scratch_params.request_sh_amortization = features->enable_sh_amortization (publisher.cpp:2558), checked against the admitted capacity (tile_renderer.cpp:584-590) [V].

    11. It is on in every named quality tier, with divisors 2–8 (core/quality_tier_config.cpp:43-44, :61-62, ...), because tier_apply_pipeline_toggles defaults to true (gaussian_splat_manager.cpp:1162) [V].

    13. A node admitted into reused pages reads the evicted tenant's colour for each splat whose phase does not match, for up to divisor − 1 frames (7 at tier low), at full opacity.
    14. The node is drawn at full opacity: DrawnNodeGPU.fade is "written as 1.0 and not read yet" (gs_instance_layout.glsl:123) [V].
    15. Under thrash (#1483: 25,523 evictions in one run, from the deviation report), this happens continuously.
    16. It needs no wind and no relocation, so #1499's statement that none of its items is live on master does not hold for the SH cache under amortization.

    18. Same class, smaller [I]:

    6.5.3 Decision [P]¶

    • Content-following caches are invalidated on (re)assignment.
    • Whenever residency assigns an atlas run to a node for an upload, or as a compaction destination, the renderer clears sh_color_cache (and the subpixel history) for exactly that record range. The clear goes into the same command stream as the upload, before the first draw that references it.
    • 0 is already the "not cached" sentinel (tile_binning.glsl:1447) [V]. This costs one buffer_clear of 4 × count bytes per upload [I].
    • A tag buffer was rejected: it costs +4 B per atlas record and per-read compares. Identity keying was rejected: there is no dense identity index.
    • Identity values do not use the address.
    • Wind and effectors use rest position (§6.3.1).
    • The sort tie-break becomes hash(node_stable_index, local_index), where local_index = atlas_index − node.first_record. The node index is in DrawnNodeGPU.chunk_id (gs_instance_layout.glsl:122) [V]. The node's first record comes from the chunk meta [I]. For instance rows the hash adds the instance's stable placement key [ADR §14.1].
    • Relation to #1499:
    • This ADR closes item 1 by splitting it. Content-following state follows the address and is invalidated on assignment. Identity state no longer depends on the address.
    • This is also the precondition for compaction of drawn nodes (#1498), and it fixes the live SH case now.
    • Items 2 (per-view begin_frame) and 3 (finish_upload without a GPU fence) stay in #1499. The cache clear only needs submission order, not a fence [I].
    • #1245 (instances sharing cache entries; view direction mixed): related but separate. The recommended fix there is to bypass the cache for asset records referenced by more than one row. It is not decided here.

    6.6 Rigid moving objects (D6)¶

    6.6.1 One-frame shadow lag¶

    • The cause is verified in the code comment:
    • "Forward+ renders the shadow passes of a frame BEFORE that frame's GS main view, so a shadow pass consumes the record of the previous main view: the draw list, rows and atlas the GPU still holds" (renderer/hlod_shadow_caster_plan.h:10-12) [V].
    • A moving object's splat shadow is therefore one frame behind, an offset of velocity × frame time [I].
    • Decision [P], "rows current, list previous":
    • Instance rows (transforms, overrides, wind time) are uploaded at the frame's sync point, before Godot's shadow passes.
    • The caster keeps the previous view's node list, so it needs no new cut, but reads the current rows.
    • An object that entered view this frame casts from the next frame on. This is accepted and reported as a counter.
    • Omni and spot casters are refused today (hlod_shadow_caster_plan.h:42, #1424) [V]. The same caster code is touched, so #1424 lands first (§8).
    • The caster refuses quantized payloads (hlod_shadow_caster_plan.h:44) [V]. GS-GAME-1 runs the raw payload, or that refusal is lifted in its own PR.

    6.6.2 A4 and A5 (GS-COMBINED-1, run 44532905736, from the deviation report)¶

    • A4, penetration depth order: 185 of 590 frames fail.
    • The verdict says "as variant A", so it fails on the chunk route as well [ADR, report].
    • Splats are sorted by centre, so world/object interpenetration is only approximately correct in 3DGS [I].
    • It is not caused by this ADR, and nothing here fixes it.
    • Decision [P]: triage the reference first (renderer versus CPU reference), then write a tolerance contract with a reason. GS-GAME-1 consumes that contract. It does not invent one.
    • A5, transform offset: 81 of 600 frames, 0.5–1.2 px, worst on the fastest object.
    • The hypothesis is one frame of transform latency [I, unproven].
    • If the triage confirms it, the "rows current" change in §6.6.1 is the fix, and A5 becomes acceptance row O2.
    • Both stay owned by GS-COMBINED-1. This ADR only orders them before GS-GAME-1.

    6.6.3 Object LOD and proxies: out of scope for the first gate [P]¶

    • Objects have no LOD levels: lod_count = 1, although GS_MAX_ASSET_LODS = 8 exists [ADR, HLOD ADR §1]. There are no instance proxies (HLOD ADR §6.9, stage 2+) [ADR].
    • GS-GAME-1 uses 32 objects, which the tail holds [I]. 1,000 trees or crowds would not fit.
    • That work is a follow-up package (WP8, M–L) under HLOD ADR §6.9, with its own gate. Until then, the one-route §4.5 budget rule refuses objects whole and explicitly [ADR].

    6.7 Skinning and deforming splats (D7): out of scope¶

    • Nothing renders animation today: "CPU sampling only; nothing renders it" (docs/features/animation.md:9) [V].
    • The owner kept animation and requires render wiring (D-6a, CLN-SH06; the animation render ADR is CLN-SH06-1) [ADR]. That ADR owns skinning.
    • This ADR constrains it in four ways [P]:
    • Deforming content is always an instance, never world-tree content (§6.1).
    • Deformation runs in depth_compute before the instance transform. It uses the same time sample as wind and must be applied by the caster too.
    • Bounds come from the skeleton or the control nodes, declared like D_max.
    • Per-splat skinning data lives in a side buffer of the instance region, not in the semantic word.
    • The design check's sizing (linear blend skinning with ≤ 4 bones, about 16 B per splat; SC-GS control nodes [Ext]) is input to CLN-SH06-1, not a decision here.
    • Collision proxies are D-6b and CLN-SH04 [ADR]. Islands (§6.1) are natural proxy owners.

    10. What a cheaper version would force to redo [I]¶

    • Region ID packed into painterly_meta: GSQ v1 refuses per-splat differences (gsq_codec.cpp:436-437) [V], and it collides with painterly.
    • Word stored in _padding without GSQ v2: silently zeroed by the Tier A codec, a vacuous pass.
    • Wind weight copied from the merge winner (today's behaviour for every non-moment field): coarse nodes sway like their heaviest member.
    • The region ID left out of the merge key: overrides bleed across regions at coarse LOD. Fixing that later means a rebake.
    • The seed moved to (NodeKey, index) with the jitter kept: merged splats still get new phases at every LOD switch, so wind is not LOD-stable.
    • Inflation without a declared bound: splats leave their node bounds, and the cut and culling drop them.
