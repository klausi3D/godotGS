What it does

A splatting pipeline that lives in the engine.

No round-trips to a separate viewer. Import a capture, bake it to a node, and ship it in your game — with the tools you already use.

Import any capture

Drop in .ply or .splat files and COLMAP camera poses. They land as a GaussianSplat3D node in your scene tree.

ply · splat · colmap · reality capture

Stream millions of splats

Distance-based LOD, per-chunk quantization, and a streaming queue keep multi-million-splat scenes fluid — not just in a demo, at runtime.

lod · quantization · octree chunks

Tune in the editor

Live LOD bias, opacity, spherical-harmonic degree and a color-grading bake — all in the Inspector, with the viewport updating as you drag.

inspector · color grading · live preview

Prove every claim

A single-process benchmark orchestrator and a doctest visual-compare lane back the numbers. No hand-wavy "blazing fast" — measured frames.

Try in 5 minutes

Your first splat, in three commands.

Build the editor from source on Linux, Windows, or macOS, then open the sample project and orbit a real capture. macOS needs a MoltenVK-capable Vulkan SDK.

Full build guide Compatibility matrix
bash
# 1 — build the editor
scons platform=linuxbsd target=editor -j$(nproc)

# 2 — open the sample project
./bin/godot.linuxbsd.editor.x86_64 \
  --path tests/examples/godot/test_project

# 3 — press Play, orbit your first splat
✓ scene loaded · 4.2M splats · 74 fps
